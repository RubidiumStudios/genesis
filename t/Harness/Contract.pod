=head1 NAME

Harness::Contract - readers of a dry apply's pipeline, for the plumbing contract

=head1 SYNOPSIS

  use lib 't';
  use Harness::Propagation;
  use Harness::Contract;

  my $h = contract_harness(config => {'pipeline.unredacted' => 'true'});
  my ($p, $why) = dry_pipeline($h);
  my $job  = pipeline_job($p, 'deploy-qa');
  my $task = task_step($job);

=head1 DESCRIPTION

The plumbing contract (studio C<design/plumbing-contract.md>) says, row by
row, what the compiled Concourse pipeline must hold, and each stage's rows
are asserted in their own C<t/integration-tests/genesis_ci_contract-*.t>.
They all read the same thing, the pipeline C<genesis pipeline-apply
--dry-run> prints, over the same repository, so the fixture and the readers
live here once.

The repository is a C<doomsday>-type root in a C<doomsday/> subdirectory of
its clone, with one environment, C<qa>, on the Concourse provider, with
C<pipeline.name> set and the root's embedded genesis in place. The names are
C<$Harness::Contract::ENV_NAME>, C<$TYPE> and C<$ROOT>.

=head1 FUNCTIONS

=head2 contract_harness(%opts)

Builds the repository. C<config> is a hash of C<.genesis/config> keys to set,
C<pipeline> and C<genesis> hashes of C<qa>'s C<genesis.pipeline> and
C<genesis> keys, and C<no_binary> leaves C<.genesis/bin/genesis> out.
C<envs> names the environments in place of C<qa> alone, and C<chained>
makes each the C<prior_env> of the next.

B<Returns:> the L<Harness::Propagation> harness.

B<Errors:> any of the harness's own.

B<Examples:>

  my $h = contract_harness(pipeline => {manual => 'true'});

=head2 dry_pipeline($h)

Runs C<pipeline-apply --dry-run --skip-vault> in the root and reads what it
prints as YAML.

B<Returns:> the pipeline and an empty string, or C<undef> and why: the exit
and its standard error, or the YAML error and the output.

B<Errors:> none; a failure is returned.

B<Examples:>

  my ($p, $why) = dry_pipeline($h);
  ok($p, 'the dry apply prints a pipeline') or diag($why);

=head2 pipeline_job($p, $name)

=head2 pipeline_resource($p, $name)

The job or resource of that name, or C<undef>.

B<Errors:> none.

B<Examples:>

  my $job = pipeline_job($p, 'propagate');

=head2 plan_steps($node)

Every C<get>, C<put> and C<task> step under a job or plan, flattened through
C<plan>, C<do>, C<in_parallel>, C<ensure>, C<on_success> and C<on_failure>.

B<Returns:> the steps, in plan order.

B<Errors:> none.

B<Examples:>

  my @gets = grep {$_->{get}} plan_steps($job);

=head2 task_step($job)

The job's first task step, or C<undef>.

B<Errors:> none.

B<Examples:>

  my $run = task_step($job)->{config}{run};

=head2 branch_resource($p)

The environment's branch resource, C<qa-branch> (contract a1), or C<undef>.

B<Errors:> none.

B<Examples:>

  is(branch_resource($p)->{source}{branch}, 'qa/doomsday');

=cut
