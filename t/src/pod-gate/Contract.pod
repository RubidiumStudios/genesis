=head1 NAME

Contract - error-path fixture for contract blocks

=head1 DESCRIPTION

Every sub is documented and correctly filed. What is wrong is what the
entries say about parameters, errors, examples and arity.

=head1 METHODS

=head2 raises_undocumented($path)

    $c->raises_undocumented($path);

Checks a path. Dies when it cannot be reached, and says nothing about it.

B<Parameters:>

=over 4

=item C<$path>

The path.

=back

B<Returns:> True.

B<Examples:>

    $c->raises_undocumented("/tmp");
    # Returns: 1

=head2 takes_args_without_examples($first, $second)

    my $joined = $c->takes_args_without_examples($first, $second);

Joins two strings. Documented parameters but no worked example.

B<Parameters:>

=over 4

=item C<$first>

The first string.

=item C<$second>

The second string.

=back

B<Returns:> The two strings joined.

=head2 example_without_outcome($v)

    my $up = $c->example_without_outcome($v);

Upper-cases a string.

B<Parameters:>

=over 4

=item C<$v>

The string.

=back

B<Returns:> The string, upper-cased.

B<Examples:>

    $c->example_without_outcome("hello");

=head2 wrong_arity($one, $two, $three)

    my $pair = $c->wrong_arity($one, $two, $three);

Takes two arguments; the heading and signature both claim three.

B<Parameters:>

=over 4

=item C<$one>

First.

=item C<$two>

Second.

=back

B<Returns:> An arrayref of both.

B<Examples:>

    $c->wrong_arity(1, 2);
    # Returns: [1, 2]
